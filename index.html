NBA Blitz

7-on-7 arcade football with NBA stars. Build your seven, then play or sim the season. No penalties, no mercy.

Six balanced teams, auto-drafted from the top 42 players in a snake draft, with every player at his best spot. Pick your team and an opponent, then play.

Your seven each plays offense and defense

Team ratings

Move arrows or WASD. Turbo Shift. Spin / Lunge Space. Offense 1-4 picks a play, then 1-3 throws to a receiver and 4 to the back. Defense pick a scheme, then steer the defender nearest the ball. 30 yards for a first down. Three good plays puts you ON FIRE.
